What is FeelyTalk?

FeelyTalk is a place to talk with friendly, supportive hosts through chat, voice or video — whichever way you feel comfortable.

How do I create an account?

Sign up with a display name and choose an avatar. You can also set your interests, which is optional.

Is FeelyTalk free to use?

Downloading the app and creating your profile is free. Some features, such as longer calls with hosts, may need credits or a subscription. Any price is always shown in the app before you pay, so there are no surprises.

How do I become a host?

Create an account, set up your profile with your interests and talking style, then go online. See the Join Us page for the steps.

How do I make payments or receive earnings?

Users add credits or choose a plan in the app and pay with the payment methods shown at checkout. Hosts can see their earnings in the app and request a payout to their linked bank account or UPI ID. Payout timelines and minimum amounts are listed in the app’s payments section.

Is my personal information safe?

Your personal information stays private and secure. Read more on our Safety page.

Can I stay anonymous?

You join with a display name and an avatar of your choice, so you don’t need to use your real name.

What kind of conversations can I have?

Casual chats, deep talks or just random fun conversations — as long as they stay friendly and respectful.

What should I do if I face an issue?

Report, block or mute the user right from the conversation, and contact our support team if you need more help.
