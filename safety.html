Our community rules

Keep It Friendly and Respectful

To make FeelyTalk a safe and enjoyable place for everyone, we have a few simple rules.

  • Be Respectful

    Treat hosts and other users with kindness and respect.

  • No Harassment

    No bullying, stalking, threats or repeated unwanted messages.

  • No Abuse or Hate Speech

    Do not use abusive language, hate speech or discriminate against anyone.

  • 18+

    Keep It 18+

    FeelyTalk is for 18+ users only. Do not share or ask for underage content.

  • No Explicit Content

    Do not share explicit, illegal or harmful content.

  • Respect Boundaries

    If someone is not comfortable, stop the conversation.

  • No Scams or Fraud

    Do not share false information, promote scams or ask for money outside the platform.

  • Follow Local Laws

    Use FeelyTalk in accordance with applicable laws in India.
